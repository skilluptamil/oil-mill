Core Manufacturing

Commercial Cold-Pressed Oil Production

We manufacture premium culinary and therapeutic grade plant oils adhering strictly to the FSSAI and Codex Alimentarius virgin oil specifications.

Our temperature monitors ensure that oil temperature during processing never exceeds 40°C. We maintain rigorous lot separation, ensuring that groundnut, sesame, coconut, and mustard oils run in dedicated, sanitized stainless lines without cross-contamination.

  • Dedicated lines for allergen control (peanuts segregated).

UV-Protected Storage

Hygienic Bottling & Custom Packaging

Packaging cold-pressed oils is an art. Because our oils lack artificial chemical preservatives, they are vulnerable to photo-oxidation if stored in clear plastic.

We provide precision volumetric filling into amber glass bottles, flint glass jars with tamper-proof wooden corks, food-grade tinplate containers, and export-compliant epoxy-lined steel drums.

  • Nitrogen-flushed capping to eliminate headspace oxygen.
  • Automated induction sealing and tamper-evident shrink sleeves.
  • Sizes from 250ml sample bottles to 15L commercial tins.
