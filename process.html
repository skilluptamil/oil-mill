Our Traditional Extraction Process

A step-by-step masterclass in how traditional wooden Chekku mechanics retain pure life-force in every drop of oil.

Home / Manufacturing Process
Stage 01

Selection of Superior Native Seeds

The foundation of pure oil begins in fertile soil. We partner directly with certified dry-land organic farmers across Pollachi, Dharmapuri, and Madurai to procure indigenous seed varieties.

We avoid genetically modified hybrids bred for artificial volume. Instead, we harvest native Tamil groundnuts, deep black micro-sesame, and naturally matured coconuts cured in sea breezes under the sun.

  • Non-GMO verified native heritage seeds.
  • Strict moisture calibration between 5% and 7%.
  • Zero chemical fumigation during silo storage.
Stage 02

Manual Winnowing & Destoning

Before entering the wooden mortar, seeds undergo traditional manual winnowing and multi-level mechanical sieving.

This critical step eliminates field dust, chaff, broken skins, hollow seeds, and quartz micro-pebbles. We never wash seeds with water or chlorine solutions, which would induce rancid moisture reactions inside the extracted oil.

99.8% Seed Purity Guarantee
Triple-sieved through food-grade stainless screens.
Stage 03

Traditional Vaagai Chekku Extraction

The heart of our mill. Clean seeds are loaded into a monolithic granite stone mortar. A rotating pestle carved from seasoned Vaagai wood (Albizia Lebbeck) crushes the seeds with patient, slow rotary friction.

Turning at only 14 RPM, the wood absorbs frictional warmth, keeping the mash below 40°C. For sesame oil, organic Karupatti (palm jaggery) is introduced at precise intervals to temper bitterness and infuse vital organic trace minerals.

Stage 04

Natural Sedimentation & Sunlight Settling

Freshly pressed oil contains microscopic seed pulp fibers. Rather than forcing the oil through high-pressure industrial separators or using chemical flocculants, we employ natural gravity.

The golden oil rests in food-grade stainless steel settling tanks beneath greenhouse glass roofs for 48 to 72 hours. Gentle ambient warmth and gravity cause micro-solids to settle naturally to the bottom, preserving all natural sterols and aromatics in the clear upper layer.

Stage 05

Unbleached Cotton Cloth Filtration & Lab Audits

The clear oil is decanted from the settling tanks and filtered through multiple layers of hand-spun, unbleached pure cotton cloths.

Every production batch is sampled by our in-house lab chemist. We verify:

  • Free Fatty Acid (FFA): Guaranteed under 1.0% (proves fresh, unfermented seeds).
  • Peroxide Value (PV): Under 5 meq O2/kg (guarantees zero oxidation/rancidity).
  • Adulteration Screening: Negative for mineral oil, argemone oil, or castor cross-mix.
Stage 06

UV-Shielded Bottling & Safe Nationwide Delivery

The finished oil is packaged into pharmaceutical-grade dark amber glass bottles and sealed tinplate containers.

We deliberately reject cheap plastic bottles that leach endocrine-disrupting phthalates into pure fats. Finished packs are nitrogen-flushed, induction-sealed, and dispatched in shock-resistant honeycomb paper packaging right to your door.
