Technical Specifications & Quality Parameters

Contract pressing of edible oils demands meticulous engineering. In modern high-friction mills, seeds heat up uncontrollably, turning delicate fats into harmful trans-isomers and oxidants. Here is how our contract facility safeguards your product:

Real-time Infrared Temperature Auditing

Each Vaagai wood pestle is monitored with non-contact infrared sensors. If seed mash temperature approaches 39°C, machine speed drops automatically to allow heat dissipation into the wood grain.

Strict Non-Allergenic Cleaning Cycles

Between different oil seeds (e.g. switching from Peanut/Groundnut to Sesame or Coconut), mortars undergo thorough mechanical scraping, organic steam sanitization, and swab testing to prevent allergen transfer.

Natural Sun Sedimentation Chambers

Instead of using harsh filter presses that tear plant cell walls, we transfer freshly pressed oil into glass-lined stainless settling tanks under filtered sunlight for 48 hours. Micro-sediments drop naturally to the conical bottom.

Clear Workflow

6-Stage Contract Milling Workflow

From receiving raw seed harvest to freight delivery of finished, labeled bottles.

01

1. Seed Intake & Moisture Check

Moisture level is measured (target 5% - 7%). Seeds are sieved, destoned, and weighed accurately on calibrated scales.

02

2. Vaagai Chekku Crushing

Cold-pressing under dedicated Vaagai pestles at 14 RPM. Organic palm jaggery is added upon client request.

03

3. Gravity Sun Sedimentation

Raw oil rests in food-grade SS 316 conical tanks for 48 hours where heavy mucilage and seed fibers naturally settle.

04

4. Fine Cotton Cloth Filtration

Oil passes through unbleached multi-layered pure cotton filters. Zero diatomaceous earth or acid bleaching.

05

5. In-House Lab Testing

Our quality chemist tests each batch for free fatty acid (FFA), peroxide value, and moisture before release.

06

6. Bottling, Labeling & Dispatch

Filled into client's chosen amber bottles or tins, sealed with tamper bands, packed into sturdy master cartons, and shipped.
