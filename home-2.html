Artisanal Engineering

The Vaagai Wood Distinction: Why Wood Matters

The traditional South Indian Chekku consists of a monolithic hard granite mortar paired with a heavy rotating pestle carved exclusively from aged Vaagai wood (Albizia Lebbeck).

Thermal Absorption Capacity

Unlike modern steel expellers that conduct heat and fry the seed, porous Vaagai wood absorbs friction heat dynamically, maintaining oil extraction below 40°C.

Ultra-Slow 14 RPM Rotation

Our pestles cycle at only 14 revolutions per minute. This delicate pace prevents mechanical cell breakage and stops free fatty acid degradation.

Natural Palm Jaggery Catalyst

In our sesame oil pressing, Karupatti (palm jaggery) is added to bind residual astringency, naturally infusing trace minerals without synthetic clarifiers.

Nutritional Integrity

The Real Benefits of Unrefined Cold-Pressed Oils

Transparent, scientifically verifiable dietary advantages when you switch from industrial solvent-extracted fats to pure cold-pressed lipids.

Active Natural Vitamin E

Because no high temperatures are applied during extraction, fat-soluble antioxidants including alpha-tocopherols remain bio-available to combat free radicals.

Balanced Fatty Acid Profile

Retains the natural equilibrium of Monounsaturated (MUFA) and Polyunsaturated (PUFA) essential fatty acids that support healthy lipid metabolism.

Zero Chemical Solvents

Commercial oils are washed with petrochemical hexane and bleached with diatomaceous clay. Our oils touch only wood, granite, and organic cotton.

Authentic Flavour & Aroma

Experience unadulterated taste. Dishes prepared with true chekku oils require significantly lower volume because the authentic essence is so potent.

Our Strict 4-Pillar Quality Promise

We operate transparently. Every batch pressed in our mill carries a traceable lot number and undergoes stringent testing before bottling.

1. Direct Farmer Seed Sourcing

We do not buy stale seed stock from brokers. We partner directly with organic farmer clusters, paying fair trade premiums for sun-cured harvest.

2. Acid Value & Peroxide Testing

Every batch is tested for free fatty acid (FFA < 1.0%) and low peroxide value, guaranteeing zero rancidity or oxidation.

3. Food-Grade UV-Shield Bottling

We deliberately avoid cheap single-use plastic bottles. We package in tinted dark amber glass and sealed tinplate containers to prevent light oxidation.

4. Open Mill Policy

Visitors are welcome to step into our Erode mill any working day to observe the wooden chekkus turning and taste fresh oil straight from the spout.
