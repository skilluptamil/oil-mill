Pure Wooden Chekku

A Journey of Unaltered Purity from Village to Bottle

Founded with an unwavering dedication to traditional wellness, Vaagai Chekku Oil Mill specializes exclusively in unrefined, authentic wooden Marachekku cold-pressed edible oils.

Modern high-speed industrial expellers heat oil seeds to over 140°C, stripping essential fatty acids and necessitating petroleum solvents like hexane for artificial extraction. We take a different path: we let seasoned granite stone mortars and dense Vaagai wood pestles gently cold-press native sun-dried seeds.

Legacy of 3 Generations

From Bullock-Driven Wooden Press to Modern Standard

How our family preserved traditional Tamil oil making through changing decades.

1988 – The Bullock Press

Our grandfather began with two bullock-driven wooden chekkus in Sathyamangalam, crushing native groundnuts and black sesame for surrounding agricultural hamlets.

2008 – Slow-Motor Innovation

We designed custom reduction gearboxes that replicate the exact 14 RPM speed of bullock chekkus, preserving cold extraction without animal fatigue.

Present – ISO & Export Excellence

Today, Vaagai Chekku supplies households, Ayurvedic physicians, and export partners worldwide while keeping our sacred zero-chemical commitment untouched.

Our Purpose

Our Mission

To eradicate chemically refined, bleached, and deodorized oils from modern kitchens by making authentic, affordable, and laboratory-verified cold-pressed oils accessible to every home.

  • Direct fair pricing for traditional organic farmers.
  • Zero chemical refinement, zero synthetic stabilizers.
  • Complete batch-level lab transparency for consumer peace of mind.
Future Horizon
