Our Mill Story

Rooted in Tradition, Extracted for Modern Health

At Vaagai Chekku, we believe true nutrition is never rushed. Unlike factory expellers that generate scorching friction heat exceeding 120°C and use chemical solvents like hexane, our artisanal wooden pestles turn at a gentle 14 revolutions per minute.

The friction absorbed by indigenous Albizia Lebbeck (Vaagai Wood) keeps extraction temperatures below 40°C, ensuring that essential antioxidants, natural aroma, polyphenols, and Vitamin E remain 100% active.

Seed to Bottle

Our Ancient 6-Step Extraction Process

Experience the honest journey of our oils from organic farming communities to pristine glass bottles.

01

1. Native Seed Selection

We source only sun-dried, non-GMO native seeds directly from certified smallholder farmers across Tamil Nadu and Karnataka.

02

2. Manual Winnowing & Cleaning

Seeds are triple-cleaned and air-winnowed to eliminate dust, immature pods, and pebbles without chemical washing.

03

3. Slow Vaagai Chekku Pressing

Seeds are crushed inside our stone mortar with a heavy Vaagai wood pestle at a calm pace of 14 RPM with palm jaggery.

04

4. Natural Sun Sedimentation

The extracted oil rests in food-grade stainless tanks under natural sunlight for 48 hours to let heavy sediment settle naturally.

05

5. Unbleached Cotton Filtration

Clear golden oil is strained through unbleached organic cotton cloths. No acid washing, no deodorizing, no bleaching agents.

06

6. Hygienic Bottling & Dispatch

Sealed in UV-resistant glass bottles and food-grade tins, ready for wholesale shipment or doorstep delivery to your home.
